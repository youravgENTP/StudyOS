# Dashboard

The Dashboard is a projection and owns no separate business records. Its Tasks card reads open tasks, its Routine card reads today's durable routine progress, its Habits card reads today's scheduled behavior completions, and its Caffeine card derives current and bedtime estimates from intake records. Other cards continue to use representative data until their feature slices exist.
